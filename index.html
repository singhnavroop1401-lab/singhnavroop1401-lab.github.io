Production Scheduling & Lot-Sizing Study

Modelled a six-product, five-stage line — Press → Laser → Bending → Profiling → Welding, with product-specific skip paths and a two-machine Profiling bottleneck — in Tecnomatix Plant Simulation, then benchmarked five order-release strategies over 100 orders / 1,050 units against makespan, total tardiness and on-time delivery.

Search
A lot-based search over lot size and release sequence cut makespan 20% and tardiness 64% against the family-production baseline
Finding
EDD — the simplest rule tested — eliminated tardiness entirely and won the weighted objective outright, because per-order release buys perfect delivery reliability at the cost of a set-up explosion
Plan
Lot-based release now, EDD after a SMED set-up-reduction programme

  • SimTalk
  • Scheduling & Lot-Sizing

Summer Semester 2026 · deterministic demand — one fixed-seed realisation of 100 orders, identical across all runs.
