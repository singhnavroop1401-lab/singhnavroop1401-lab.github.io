01 About

The interesting problems
live between components.

Sheet01 TitleAbout BasedMagdeburg, DE PlantAug 2023 — Sep 2025

My first two years out of university were spent on a polysilicon plant site in Jamnagar, watching three reciprocating compressors go in. Drawings that looked fine on paper met concrete that had already been poured. The hard part was never inside a component — it was the interfaces, the sequence, and the things nobody had written down.

That's what pulled me toward systems engineering, and it's what I'm studying at Otto von Guericke University Magdeburg — MBSE, system architecture, and simulating a production line before anyone builds it. I still like being the person who can read the P&ID and the model.

Looking for a working-student role where both halves of that are useful — anywhere in Germany, and happy to relocate.
